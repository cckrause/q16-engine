The basic steps to construct the house.

1. start with new sector use that as the bottom floor inside,next make a door way
,window hole,then the bottom part of the porch.

2. next the second floor is made,cut a hole for the door ,and add the porch top.

3. back to the bottom floor insert a wall extrude sectors for the stairs.
 
4. add the top floor in two pieces

5. start with a new sector "K" insert a bunch of vertices "INS"  and wrap it around the house to build the outside area.

6. Adjoin the out side walls . making sure to select the wall pointing to the last sector.

7. optional create the sloped subsectors,to get to roof,(only did this so you could have a look)could have made stairs too.
